# Benchmark results

Abbreviations: ZP = Labour Code, ZZ = Employment Act, ZNP = Sickness Insurance Act, NV 590 = Government Decree 590/2006. Questions, answers and manual review notes quoted below are in Czech, as in the data files.

Generated by `tools/experiments.py` + `tools/make_docs.py`. Run from commit `531da20` (uncommitted changes), 2026-10-16T02:59:05, offline replay from cache ($0), took 36.3 s, cost $0.00. Python 3.13.14, numpy 2.5.3, Windows AMD64.

**What is measured and what is not.** Retrieval is replayed here from stored embeddings and stored LLM answers (rewrite, selection). The retrieval code is therefore verified and the run is repeatable, but **no new model call was made**. The answers (section 4) come from earlier measurements with manual review. What a section does not say is stated in that section.

## 2. What each part contributes (ablation)

Paired comparison on the same questions. "A better / B better" = on how many questions A, resp. B, had the higher Recall@5. p is an exact two-sided sign test: p below 0.05 says the difference is probably not chance. Above that it is only an observation.

| Comparison | A better | B better | tie | p |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| RRF v2 × e5-large only | 4 | 5 | 47 | 1.000 |
| RRF v2 × RRF without rewrite | 8 | 3 | 45 | 0.227 |
| RRF v2 × RRF without BM25 | 3 | 5 | 48 | 0.727 |
| RRF v2 × weighted sum | 2 | 4 | 50 | 0.688 |
| v3 (LLM selection) × RRF v2 | 9 | 0 | 47 | 0.004 |
| e5-large only × e5-base | 5 | 2 | 49 | 0.453 |

What follows from this (numbers are from the tables above):

- **LLM selection is the only component with a clear benefit.** Recall@1 0.54 → 0.85, MRR 0.71 → 0.96, better on 9 questions, worse on 0.
- **BM25 did not pay off on these questions.** RRF without BM25 has Recall@5 0.88 and Recall@20 0.99, RRF with BM25 0.86 and 0.96. The difference is not statistically significant, but the direction is the opposite of what was expected. The set contains no questions about exact section numbers and identifiers, which is exactly where BM25 is supposed to be strong. The decision is therefore postponed to a new set (see [limitations.md](limitations.md)).
- **The question rewrite does no harm without BM25.** e5-large alone: Recall@5 0.86; with the rewrite: 0.88. The difference is about two questions and cannot be told apart from chance.
- **e5-large vs e5-base:** Recall@5 0.86 vs 0.80.
- **A lay question hurts BM25:** 0.58 vs 0.76 after the rewrite. Here the rewrite clearly helps (the law says "mzda", not "výplata"; both are Czech words for pay).

The value of k barely shows in the result: the intervals overlap. That is why the default 60 stays.

### RRF vs weighted score sum

The BM25 weight was chosen on dev questions only (by MRR): **0.05**. On all questions the weighted sum has Recall@5 0.88 and MRR 0.76, RRF 0.86 and 0.71. The difference is not significant (p = 0.69). RRF stays because it needs no weight tuning and no score normalisation. The optimal BM25 weight came out very low, which is the same signal as above: keyword matching helps little.

The LLM also sees all 20 candidates at once and is paid for on every query. The cross-encoder is free and deterministic, but slow on CPU.

**What this does not show.** 66 of 66 for v3 is inflated: 56 of these questions were used for tuning. The honest check is the "test" row (10 questions, interval lower bound ~0.72), and it alone does not prove that the system will do the same on new questions. **Number check.** The column "numbers outside cited chunks" is a suspicion, not an error: a number in the answer that is not in the text of any cited chunk. The check distinguishes two cases: a number that is in another supplied chunk than the cited one (wrong citation), and a number that is in none (a derived value, e.g. a sum or a half, or invented). Numbers written in words in the law ("patnáctidenní", i.e. fifteen-day) are converted to digits.

Suspicions for v3 (3 answers):

| Question | Number in another supplied chunk (wrong citation) | Number in no chunk (derived?) | Cited chunks |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7: Kolik hodin týdně můžu pracovat na dohodu o pracovní činnosti? | 20 | – | [1, 3, 4] |
| 20: Kolik peněz dostanu, když budu na nemocenské? | – | 15 | [1, 2, 3, 4] |
| 21: Kolik hodin volna v kuse musím mít aspoň jednou za týden? | 11 | 35 | [1, 2, 3, 5] |

Manual walk-through of these answers: a sum (24 + 11 = 35 hours), a half (20 hours out of 40) and "from the 15th day" after 14 days are correct derived values that the answer itself explains, but they cannot be backed by a citation. **One real citation error:** for question 21, "11 hours" is in chunk [4], the answer cites it as [1]. The answer is correct in substance, only the reference points elsewhere. An AI judge that compares meaning would easily miss this.
